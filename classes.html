Entry Requirements

Where Each Level Starts.

Each level states what you need to have done first. If you are unsure where you fit, ask and the school will advise.

Level 01 · Beginner

No experience needed

The beginner course assumes you have never baked bread or pastry.

Level 02 · Intermediate

Beginner complete, or equivalent

Completed the beginner level, or you are comfortable with a basic yeast dough.

Level 03 · Advanced

Intermediate complete, or equivalent

Completed the intermediate level, or you are confident with enriched, laminated and wheat doughs.

Bundles

Take the Whole Pathway.

Any two classes in the same track are 15% off; all three are 25% off. The discount is applied automatically in your booking summary and again when your booking is priced.

Discounts apply to classes in the same track only. Mixing a bread class with a pastry class does not trigger a bundle, and buying bakery products does not affect the class price.

What Classes Cover

Skills That Travel Home.

The classes are built around method rather than demonstration, so that what you learn can be repeated in your own kitchen afterwards.
