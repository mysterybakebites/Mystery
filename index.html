One Kitchen, Two Things

The Bakehouse and the School.

Mystery Bakebite sells the finished bake and teaches how to make it. Both start from the same recipes, the same standards and the same kitchen.

The Bakehouse

Cakes, doughnuts, slices, loaves, cookies, cupcakes, parfait and savoury treats, made to order in small batches. Custom celebration cakes from a bento gift to a three-tier wedding cake.

Custom orders begin with an occasion and a date, not a product code.

The Baking School

Six practical courses across two tracks, three levels each. Bread and pastry taught in person in small groups, one week per level, so the skills travel home to your own kitchen.

The person who designs a cake is the person who bakes it, and the person who teaches it.
